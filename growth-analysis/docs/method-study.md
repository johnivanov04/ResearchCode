## 3. Quantification limit on the synthetic sample plate

The sample plate model (see `synthetic.py`) with 150 different seeds; credible wells only. *Noise-free / r* is the method's estimate on the same curve without noise (using the mean quantification limit of the noisy plates); it is the right reference for bias, because the method reports an average rate over the exponential phase, which is below r for logistic growth. *Bias* is the mean estimate relative to that noise-free value.
